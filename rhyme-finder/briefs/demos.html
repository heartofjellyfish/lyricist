Corpus redesign — pick a direction

Three takes on How songwriters rhyme

Same data, same design tokens (xuan paper · sumi ink · vermilion accent · Cormorant + Spectral + DM Mono). Different reading models. Click in, scroll, expand a few groups, and tell me which one feels right.

A Ledger compact · scannable

Each group collapses to one tight row: partner word, song count, and a peek of the first instance. Scan ten rhymes in one glance, expand the ones you want. Closest in spirit to the current row-list — feels like a footnote index in a scholarly edition.

B Folio typographic · book-like

Each group opens with a 40px Cormorant italic partner word — like a chapter heading. Generous vertical rhythm; the page reads as a sequence of small entries. Slower, more deliberate; rewards reading top-to-bottom rather than skimming.

C Atlas overview + detail

A frequency strip at the top names every partner word inline, sized by how often it appears. One glance: you see screen and clean are the clichés, kerosene is the gem. Click any word in the strip to jump down to its group. Two reading modes — survey, then dive.
